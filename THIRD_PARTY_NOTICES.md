## Components shipped in release artifacts

Each Modula Deploy release (`downloads.usemodula.org`) contains, next to our own
binaries, these unmodified upstream programs. `scripts/release.sh` builds Caddy and
`scripts/fetch-build-tools.sh` fetches BuildKit, Railpack and WAL-G, checked against
pinned SHA-256 sums.
